A Letter to KC

wimze

Notes:

Hingang malalim medyo horror.

Chapter 1

Hi,

I wanted to tell you this in person sana, but I just couldn’t seem to find the right timing. Or maybe I was just waiting for the courage to do so.

I developed feelings for you.

Kailan pa? I honestly don’t know. I couldn’t pinpoint the exact moment it happened, and I don’t think there was ever one particular moment. Maybe it was a collection of little things??

Maybe it started when I caught myself thinking of you agad whenever I saw sanrio characters, miffy or anything cute, like charms and stickers. Or whenever someone brought up Hamilton, musicals, theater, AO3, Disney, or EXO. Maybe it was when I started finding your clumsiness endearing, or when I caught myself getting a little too excited whenever your name pops up in my notifs. When I started wanting to know how your day was going, what you were up to, and what you were going through.

Or maybe it was when you started involving me in the apps you were into, or when I found myself wanting to try the foods you like.

And perhaps I should admit that I get a little too entertained whenever you playfully call me baby, even though I’m fully aware that you call all your friends those sweet words, and probably even sweeter ones.

Somewhere along the way, the little things about you started to mean a little more than they should.

I don’t know why, but I really like hearing you laugh. And I’d be lying if I said I didn’t enjoy our little moments of eye contact. I swear, I could stare at you 24/7. That’s probably why I prefer sitting across from you rather than beside you niyahahahehe T_T

And then there’s the way you treat me.

You open doors for me, guide me around, and sometimes even speak for me when I feel like the most introverted person in the entire world. I feel safe around you, and I don’t think I’ve ever properly expressed how much I appreciate that.

Baka mag p-pause ka dito, you would probably argue that these are just basic human decency. In that case, congrats, lumaki ka nang tama! HAHAHAHA

And speaking of all these little things, I think I started becoming a little too conscious of my feelings during the early days of your CPALE season. I remember wondering if it was okay to send you good luck messages for three consecutive days. Like, would that be too much? Would you think anything of it? Kaya first and last day lang ako nag-send because I was afraid you might read too much into it. (me lang naka-isip non, right? nasa denial stage pa kasi. Sorry, may utang akong isang good luck message BAHAHAHA)

Looking back, maybe that was one of the first times I became a little too conscious of how I expressed my affection towards you.

And I finally started acknowledging what I felt when I stopped calling you ate kc.

Maybe that was when the line between friendship and something more began to blur, at least on my end.

But what I feel for you has grown into something much deeper than I initially expected.

At some point, I found myself wanting more than just the occasional conversations and little moments we shared. I wanted to know you better. I wanted to be part of more of your days, to hear more of your stories, and to discover the parts of you I hadn’t had the chance to see yet.

I find myself craving more of whatever it is that we have. I want to get involved. I want to be there, not just as someone who happens to be around, but as someone who genuinely wants to be part of your life.

You might be wondering, ano bang meron sa’yo?

Other people admire you for your beauty, and honestly, can’t blame them. But the more I got to know you, the more I found myself drawn to the things that make you who you are.

I like how you can be so bubbly and playful, but also gentle and thoughtful. I like the way you treat people, how you show respect, and how you never seem to make a big deal out of the good things you do. You have your own principles, and there’s something about the way you carry yourself that I find admirable.

I’ve tried to stop these feelings, too. I stopped spamming you on tiktok. I stopped calling you by those endearments that probably shouldn’t be used between friends. I even tried ghosting you… I’m sorry for that. That was probably one of the lamest things I’ve ever done.

Akala ko kasi makakalaya ako sa feelings ko kapag ginawa ko yon. I thought that if I distanced myself enough, everything would go back to how it used to be. But no. Mas lumala lang.

So eventually, I just let myself fall.

Sometimes, I wonder if I’m simply romanticizing everything or reading too much into the little things. If I’m giving meaning to gestures that were never meant to mean anything more. #delulu

And perhaps that’s what makes this so difficult.

I’m torn between telling you how I feel and letting these feelings fade away on their own. Kasi alam ko kung ano ang pwedeng mangyari. I’ve experienced this before, and you know that. Things might change. Things might become awkward. And there’s always the possibility of losing something we’ve spent years building.

Pero parang ikamamatay ko naman ata kapag hindi ko to sinabi.

I don’t want to keep hiding my feelings in playlists anymore, secretly hoping that maybe, one day, you’ll notice the songs and somehow figure out what I’ve been trying to tell you all along. I don’t want to keep leaving pieces of my feelings in places where you might stumble upon them, only to pretend they were never meant for you in the first place.

So, here I am, finally putting them into words.

I like you. More than a friend.

And honestly, hindi ko na alam kung anong gagawin ko sa lahat ng feelings na to. Tho aware ako na you like someone else, I still want to ask you this, just this once. And I hope you’ll be honest.

Do you think there’s a chance for us to take this friendship to the next stage?

You don’t have to tell me what you think I want to hear. You don’t have to soften your answer just because you might be afraid of hurting me. I would rather know how you genuinely feel, even if it’s not what I’m hoping for.

Whatever your answer may be, I hope you know that I genuinely value you and the friendship we’ve built all these years.

But at the same time, I don’t want to keep pretending that I don’t feel anything. I don’t want to look back someday and wonder what might have happened if I had just been a little braver.

I guess, more than anything, I just wanted you to know.

And whatever happens after this, I’m glad I got to know you the way I did :)

End Notes:

Thank you for reading.
